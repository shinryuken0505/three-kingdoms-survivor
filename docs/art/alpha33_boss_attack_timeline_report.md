# Alpha.33 Boss Attack Timeline

- Boss specials now use explicit wind-up, impact and recovery timing.
- Zhang Jiao and Zhang Liang resolve lightning circles sequentially.
- Gao Shun and Zhang He physically charge along the locked warning line and can hit only once.
- Hua Xiong uses a timed sector impact; Lu Bu performs a two-stage sweep.
- Normal boss updates pause while a special timeline is active.
- Damage, impact visuals, sound and screen shake resolve on the same event frame.
- Every completed special grants a visible recovery/counter window.

Manual gameplay QA remains required for damage balance, collision feel and every boss profile.
